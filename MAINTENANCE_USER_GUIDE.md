# Maintenance User Guide

Open **Issues** to see assignments permitted by your team/category/location scope. Acknowledge promptly, start work, add notes and evidence, and use waiting statuses only with a clear explanation. Upload before/after evidence through the attachment control, then mark resolved for reporter or coordinator verification.

Do not cancel, delete or reject reports. Reopened work returns to the operational queue with its complete history. Attendance and unrelated private conversations are not available to maintenance-only accounts.
